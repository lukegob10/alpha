Inflation eased over 2024, then rose in the final quarter

US all-items consumer prices were 2.9% above a year earlier in December, down from 3.4% in December 2023. That is slower price growth, not a fall in the price level.

Coverage: US city average, CPI-U, December 2023–December 2024. This fixed historical example is not a current economic update.

On this page

Main conclusion

The year-end rate fell by 0.5 percentage points, but the path was uneven. September’s 2.4% was the low in this period; December ended 0.5 points above it. Use the full series when describing the year rather than treating the two endpoints as a continuous decline.

What the data cannot establish

A national consumer basket does not measure any individual household’s expenses. Annual changes compare different rolling 12-month windows; they do not isolate the effect of a policy or explain why a particular month moved.

Rounded observations limit precision. No confidence interval is supplied in this example, and no forecast follows from these charts.

Next analytical decision

Before attributing the late-year increase, inspect the release’s component indexes and their weights. Do not add category growth rates: they are not additive contributions to the all-items rate.

Provenance and missing-data policy

The canonical values are the semantic tables embedded in this document. The line series transcribes all 13 all-items observations in Chart 2 of the archived BLS release; the bar table selects three of those same observations. No values are missing in this fixture. If an observation were absent, it would be shown as “Not available” with an empty data value, never substituted with zero.
